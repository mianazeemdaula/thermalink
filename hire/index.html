Questions

USB, network or Bluetooth?

All three. USB printers print through the computer's normal print queue, network printers on port 9100, and Bluetooth printers from Flutter apps on Android.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. The page talks to a small agent on the shop computer, so it does not need WebUSB or a browser extension.

My app is in Flutter, not on the web.

That's fine. I build printing straight into Flutter apps (Bluetooth and network printers), and into Electron and Tauri desktop apps.

Can you print labels?

Yes. I write the ZPL or TSPL for your label size, with text, barcodes and QR codes.
