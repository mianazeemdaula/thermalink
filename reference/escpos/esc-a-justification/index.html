ESC a (1B 61): center or right-align text on a receipt printer

1B 61 01 centers, 1B 61 02 right-aligns, 1B 61 00 goes back to left.

ASCIIESC a n
Hex1B 61 n
Decimal27 97 n

Parameters

n0 = left, 1 = center, 2 = right. The viewer also accepts 0x30 to 0x32 (ASCII "0" to "2").

What it does

Alignment applies to whole lines, so send it before the first character of the line. Sent in the middle of a line, it takes effect from the next line; the viewer warns about this.

It also positions barcodes, QR codes and raster images. Thermalink centers its logo and QR blocks by sending ESC a 1 before the image and ESC a 0 after it.

escpos-php's setJustification() accepts JUSTIFY_LEFT (0), JUSTIFY_CENTER (1) and JUSTIFY_RIGHT (2).

Example

Center, right, then left again.

1B 61 01 43 65 6E 74 65 72 65 64 0A 1B 61 02 52 69 67 68 74 0A 1B 61 00 4C 65 66 74 0A

Sending it from code

JavaScript (bytes)

const align = { left: [0x1b, 0x61, 0], center: [0x1b, 0x61, 1], right: [0x1b, 0x61, 2] };

PHP with escpos-php

$printer->setJustification(Printer::JUSTIFY_CENTER); // 1B 61 01

Python (raw bytes)

data = b"\x1b\x61\x01" + b"Centered\n" + b"\x1b\x61\x00"

Common mistakes

  • Switching alignment halfway through a line to right-align a price. Use spaces (or tabs) to push the price right instead; alignment is per line.
  • Forgetting ESC a 0 after a centered logo, so the item lines come out centered too.
